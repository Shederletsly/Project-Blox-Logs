Halloween 1.1

Halloween 1.1

  • Introduced the Halloween seasonal presentation across supported interface areas.
  • Expanded the Spooky visual identity with dark purple tones, orange accents, atmospheric elements, and dedicated seasonal styling.
  • Added seasonal menu and transition treatment with dedicated presentation and audio behavior.
  • Kept the Title Screen on the Restored experience while the seasonal theme is active.
  • Applied dedicated seasonal treatment to Controls and other compatible interface screens.
  • Kept gameplay isolated from global seasonal recoloring to preserve gameplay readability.
  • Included additional stability, presentation, and event-integration refinements throughout the Halloween 1.1 release cycle.
